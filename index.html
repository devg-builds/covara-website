06 — Common questions

Answers, upfront.

How does the 48-hour prototype work?

After our discovery call, we design and build a working first version of your site within 48 hours. You see your actual brand, content, and layout live — not a mock-up — before committing to the full build.

What does "managed forever" actually mean?

On the Growth plan, we handle hosting, domain renewals, security, updates, and content edits every month. You never touch a control panel. If something breaks at 2 a.m., that's our problem, not yours.

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes — completely. The domain is registered in your name and all content belongs to you. If you ever choose to leave, we hand over everything cleanly. No lock-in, ever.

Can you work with businesses outside India?

Absolutely. We serve clients in India and internationally. Calls are scheduled around your timezone and pricing can be quoted in your currency.

What if I already have a website?

We'll audit it for free and tell you honestly whether it needs a redesign, a rebuild, or just better management and SEO. Sometimes the answer is "keep it" — and we'll tell you that too.
